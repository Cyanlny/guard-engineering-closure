# Rescue, Rebase, and Handoff

Owns recurrence, rescue, behavioral rebase, generation retirement, and handoff; consumes mode/result and repair identity.

In `RESCUE`, freeze mutation, expensive starts, and overlap. Terminal budget, proposed bypass, or cross-generation recurrence alone is not loss.

## Bind recurrence and horizon

**Structural recurrence key** = closure/campaign continuity + validator/failure class + stable causal subject/interface/protocol + authority/trust + equivalent correction. Exclude generation, time, temporary paths, versions/checkers/wording; require native equivalence proof.

Bound the horizon at causal, authority, or trust breaks. Use attributable, recomputable native logs, V9 lineage, and cited evidence—not global history.

`STRUCTURAL_RECURRENCE` is an internal predicate, not mode/result/violation/state. Require native proof of two-plus generations in one closure/campaign; stable subject/protocol and equivalent or irrelevant runtime; same actionable mismatch; equivalent successful corrections with repeated attributable cost/wait/risk; and predicted recurrence.

Paths, messages, hosts, and labels prove nothing. Exclude one generation, changed/unproved causes or continuity, different roots, and required per-generation authority/seal/validation. Local repair may still win.

Compare accumulated local work with structural design, migration, compatibility, validation, authority, rollback, maintenance, and failure radius. Keep `UNKNOWN`s; choose the first sufficient lower-risk/cost authorized route. **Local progress** advances one generation; **structural progress** predicts attributable reduction across a later equivalent generation. Local success neither erases recurrence nor becomes no-progress; recurrence alone is not RESCUE.

## Freeze, classify, and choose

Preserve user work/evidence, operation/lease identity, authority, generation, budget, and signature.

Classify `SAME_ROOT` (same stable cause after one predicted correction), `NEW_ROOT` (native-evidenced/in-envelope), `VALIDATOR_DEFECT` (validator, not subject), or `INCONCLUSIVE` (one route-changing diagnostic or stop). Signatures ignore owner/package versions, wording, temporary paths, successor labels. Same root without new evidence ends retries; narrow failure never broadens scope.

After recurrence expose route-changing root/equivalence/cost unknowns and bounded observation; whether repair is wholly in-envelope; any finite boundary-contract change, authority, and one repair/evidence/authorization package with mutation frozen; and unproved continuity, boundedness, sufficient route, or reached stop. The decision owner assigns the result.

If local correction wins, record the rejected structural alternative/evidence. If the same key returns after one predicted structural repair, enter RESCUE: preserve history, forbid vN escape, and converge on one canonical owner/protocol per stable responsibility; native multiworker/multivalidator protocols may remain beneath it.

## Reconcile without erasing history

Behavioral rebase changes a finite closure/compatibility/frontier/authority-envelope/budget/baseline contract; in-envelope action is not rebase. V9 `accepted-rebaseline` is separate and, when active, requires one closure/campaign, current authority, stable writers/operations, carried history/counters, explained change, and at most one per tranche. Never reset attempts, blockers, budget, or lineage.

Complete bounded discovery; request one package. Stop on unknown authority/boundary. Require accepted rebaseline only when active V9 represents change; close before a new campaign.

## Retire generations before cleanup

After consumer-resolved current generation and native manifest agree, classify prior generations rollback-required, evidence-only, retired, or unknown. Logically retire first: remove superseded generations from compile/load/glob/module/index/manifest/runtime search; detach active symlink/consumer/writer/lease edges; mark execution-forbidden; retain required rollback/evidence outside discovery.

Immediately before isolation/deletion, re-resolve exact native identity and consumer/writer/lease/transaction edges; TOCTOU, identity change, or a new edge stops. Prefer atomic quarantine/non-discoverable retirement. Physical deletion additionally requires satisfied rollback/evidence retention and effect authority. Never delete unknown, dirty, concurrent, or unowned objects. Success is zero retired consumer reachability, not fewer files; prefer a current-only manifest/isolated root.

Replacement also requires migrated consumers/authority, stopped/transferred writers, recoverable history, and terminal/rollback evidence. Canonical owner and runtime-load edges are separate proofs: migrate consumers and validate resolution. Keep predecessors execution-forbidden; invalidate changed dependents. Approval follows native identity, never changed full-digest bytes.

Generation/version/path/checker/owner/package/snapshot/successor labels create no root, repair allowance, retry, or progress. Only native `NEW_ROOT` does. Same-root recurrence after one predicted correction enters RESCUE: freeze packages/attempts and retire successors.

## Hand off the bounded closure

Retain decision-changing acceptance, safety, authority, compatibility, scientific, and terminal evidence; otherwise merge, inline, remove, or defer. Handoff names closure/acceptance, authority/generation, envelope, writers/operations/leases, prediction, evidence/invalidation, budget, signature, wait, next/stop. For recurrence add key, horizon, equivalence, comparison, route, authorization package. Cite retrospective/accounting by evidence identity without rerunning or recreating a ledger. Omit credentials/stale projections; revalidate authority before mutation.
